  rows that are not valid Mozc TSV, removes translation/definition entries,
  normalizes English spellings, and sorts/deduplicates the remaining entries.
  The resulting adapted dictionary remains licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0. The
  attribution, source, license URL, and modification summary are also embedded
  as comment lines in the distributed dictionary itself.
